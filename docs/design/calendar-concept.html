What this concept changes

The strip scrubs the board

Drag, swipe or wheel the date strip and the board follows day by day. The highlighted window always shows exactly which days are on the board, and month starts are labelled so you can aim.

Columns land on whole days

Horizontal scrolling snaps to day columns. The arrows, Today and the strip all glide on one easing curve. A long jump, like Today from three months away, flies in over the last stretch.

The page tells you where you are

The title rolls to the next month as you cross into it. In Month view the weeks scroll continuously, and the month you are reading stays bright while its neighbours dim.

A day you can read at a glance

Picking a day opens its agenda beside the board, with booked time, the open slots between 8:00 and 19:00, and what is next today.
