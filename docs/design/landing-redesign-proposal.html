AI agents

Help that asks first.

Just say what you need. Kairos quietly hands it to the right helper, who prepares a draft for you to read. Nothing changes until you approve it — and anything you’ve locked is never shown to the AI.

Roster → tabs. Each agent plays a short exchange (question, typing, reply, artifact). Autoplays every 7s with a progress hairline; pauses on hover or focus, stops for good on the first click. Arrow keys move between agents (`role="tablist"`).

Teams & organizations

Bring everyone in with one code.

Organizations, teams and your own private space all work the same way — roles, codes and progress behave identically everywhere, so moving between them feels effortless.

Events & publishing

From a quiet plan to a full room.

When the work is ready, turn it into a public event page with your own images and details. Guests can RSVP and leave comments, and people nearby can find it.

The plan and the page live in the same place, so nothing gets copied, lost or out of date.

Live RSVP. The control works: the count rolls per digit like `ui/RollingNumber`, a guest joins the stack. The reminder and region cards sit on their own parallax depth (6–10%), and the cover’s accent arc draws in as a countdown to the date.
