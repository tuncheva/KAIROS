Proposal · 2026-09-01 · docs/ui

The notes page, brought onto
the editorial tier

Notes is a well-built surface speaking the wrong dialect. It leans on rounded-2xl cards, a gradient pill button, three stacked background tints and five different micro-type sizes between 9.5px and 13px — while the dashboard and calendar have moved to hairline rules, gap-px grids, mono micro-labels and outline-only badges. It also has, in practical terms, no motion at all: eleven popups mount and unmount raw, the mobile sheet appears without sliding, and the pane swap is a bare hidden/block toggle.

Everything below is live. Right-click a note row. Open a dialog and watch it leave. Flip the theme, cycle all six accents, and use the Motion switch to confirm every animation degrades to nothing under prefers-reduced-motion.

What changed, and why
  • One ground, three rules. The panes were bg-bg-surface / bg-bg-secondary / bg-bg-primary — three tints doing the job a border-light/60 hairline does better. All three panes now sit on bg-bg-primary, separated by rules, exactly like the dashboard's xl:border-r column split.
  • Rows, not cards. A note row was a rounded-xl card that grew a ring, a backdrop blur and a floating accent bar when selected. It is now a border-b border-border-light/45 row — the TaskRow shape — and selection is a left border that scales in from its centre plus a 7% accent wash.
  • Group headers earn their mono. "Today", "Earlier this week" and the sort pill now share one micro-label spec instead of three near-identical ad-hoc ones.
  • The title changes voice. Instrument Serif at 34px separates the thing you are writing from the chrome around it — the same move CalendarClient makes with its font-display month heading.
  • The list now moves. Rows rise on load and on every filter or sort change, staggered 30ms and capped at twelve so a long list never feels like it is loading twice. Deleting a row collapses it with dash-row-out instead of it vanishing.
  • The save indicator reads as one control. Four states cross-fade in place at 180ms rather than swapping icon and colour instantly; the Saved check draws its stroke once. Hit Save cycle above to watch dirty → saving → saved.
  • Nothing became a component. There is still no Button or Card in src/components/ui/, and this proposal does not add one — it consolidates class strings, which is the pattern the codebase already follows.

The inline strips

Two things appear inside the editor rather than as dialogs: the calendar-date picker (opened from the overflow menu) and the draft password field. Today both simply exist or do not, with no transition. They now reveal by height in 240ms and collapse the same way — the kairos-ai-widget-in treatment. Open the editor's menu in the frame above and pick Add to calendar.

Each now grows out of the corner nearest its anchor and shrinks back into it. Click the buttons; right-click the shaded strip.

What changed, and why
  • They animate. kairos-scale-in at 160ms in, a new kairos-scale-out at 120ms out. The transform-origin is set from the anchor's corner in JS, so a right-aligned menu grows from its top-right, not its centre.
  • One surface. Menu and ContextMenu both used rounded-xl bg-bg-elevated kairos-menu-surface; they now use the same rounded-xl border border-border-medium bg-bg-elevated shadow-xl as every dialog and the calendar popover. One elevation language across the whole surface.
  • Labels align with the list. MenuLabel was text-[9.5px] tracking-widest; it is now the same micro-label as the list's group headers, so "Notebook" in a menu and "Today" in the list are visibly the same kind of thing.
  • Positioning logic is kept. ContextMenu's existing clamp — MARGIN = 8, ESTIMATED = {200, 280}, re-measured in useLayoutEffect — is good and stays. Only the origin of the animation is added.
Section 03

The lock gate

Not a dialog — LockGate replaces the editor body when a locked note is opened. It is the most-seen security surface in the app and currently gives no feedback on a wrong password beyond a line of red text. Type anything and submit to see the shake; type correct to see the badge morph from error to success.

Incorrect password

What changed, and why
  • A wrong password is felt, not just read. A 400ms translateX shake on the field, three cycles, plus the error line popping in at 220ms. Nothing about the current build tells you your keystroke was rejected until you read.
  • Success is a state change, not a screen swap. The badge scales down and back (badge-morph, 300ms) while its border goes error/35 → success/40, then the editor content pops in. Today the gate is simply replaced.
  • The badge lost its fill. w-[52px] h-[52px] rounded-2xl bg-error/10 becomes a 54px outlined disc, matching every empty-state icon in §7. One shape for "a thing is in a state".
  • Copy is unchanged — notes.password.gateTitle and gateSubtitle are already good, and the "the server verifies the password for its owner alone" framing is worth keeping verbatim.
  • Reset-with-PIN stays a text button, but at micro-label scale so it reads as an escape hatch rather than a second primary action.
Section 04

The three password dialogs

These are the surfaces the codebase duplicates most: LockNoteDialog, RemoveLockDialog and PinResetDialog each hand-roll their own overlay, focus trap and card, and they disagree about which surface class to use — two take kairos-menu-surface, one takes kairos-system-card-elevated. All three are shown here on the single proposed chrome. Each opens on load; close it and the trigger is underneath.

Protect with a password — the account has a recovery PIN
Same dialog — the account has NO recovery PIN yet (the branch that grows two fields)
Remove password
Reset Password — via the recovery PIN
What changed, and why
  • One chrome, and it leaves. rounded-xl border border-border-medium bg-bg-elevated shadow-2xl on a bg-black/40 backdrop-blur-sm scrim — the CommandPalette treatment. Enter is kairos-modal-enter at 300ms; exit is a new kairos-modal-exit at 150ms. No notes dialog has an exit animation today; they unmount mid-frame.
  • Header, body, footer. Actions move to a bg-bg-surface footer with a hairline above it, so the primary action sits in the same place in all six dialogs. Today buttons are flex gap-3 mt-5 inline at the end of the form, at three different distances from the last field.
  • Labels became micro-labels. text-xs font-semibold text-fg-secondary field labels are now mono uppercase at tracking-[0.13em] — quieter than the values they label, which is the point.
  • The no-PIN branch is a real escalation. It keeps the warning-tinted block, but the block now reveals by height in 280ms rather than the dialog jumping ~120px taller between two renders.
  • Every word is kept. "The password is never stored, so without a PIN this note cannot be recovered by anyone — including us — if you forget it." is the best copy in the feature and is reproduced verbatim.
  • Five focus traps become one. Not a visual change, but the reason to do this work at all: these dialogs should mount through the existing ui/Modal + useModalBehavior, which already handles the trap, Escape, scroll lock and focus restore. See §11.
Section 05

Notebook and sharing

NotebookDialog is the simplest dialog in the feature and ShareDialog is the most complex — four stacked popover states under one field, plus a member list. Both are shown on the same chrome. In the share dialog, type into the email field: under three characters shows the org-member suggestions, an @ triggers the lookup spinner, ada@kairos.app resolves, anything else does not.

New notebook / Rename
Manage sharing — type in the email field to cycle the four popover states
What changed, and why
  • The suggestion popover stops being a second card. It was rounded-lg bg-bg-elevated kairos-system-card-elevated floating over the dialog — a card inside a card. It now attaches to the field as one continuous surface, entering with calendar-pop at 220ms.
  • Four states, one slot. Suggestions, looking-up, found, not-found all render into the same anchored container and cross-fade between each other, so the dialog height stops snapping as you type.
  • Permission is a badge, not prose. "Can Edit" / "View Only" were plain text-xs next to the name; they are now the outline badge family, so a glance down the list reads the permissions column.
  • Rows lose their fill. py-2 px-3 rounded-lg bg-bg-secondary per row becomes hairline-separated rows — same move as the note list.
  • The share list staggers in at 40ms per row when it resolves, instead of a h-10 animate-pulse block being replaced wholesale.
  • Notebook description keeps its rows={2} textarea and the maxLength={256} on the name. No behaviour is being proposed away here — only the shell.
Section 06

The three confirmations

All three come from ui/ConfirmDialog, which notes wraps. Two are destructive; the third is the one that fires after two wrong passwords and is not destructive, but currently looks almost identical to the two that are.

Delete Note — destructive
Delete notebook — destructive, but the notes survive
Incorrect password → offer the PIN reset (after RESET_PROMPT_AFTER = 2 failures)
What changed, and why
  • Destructive and non-destructive stop looking alike. The two deletes get an error/35 outlined icon and a filled bg-error confirm. The wrong-password prompt gets the neutral accent treatment and keeps "Try again" as its cancel — it is an offer, not a warning.
  • The consequence is spelled out where it differs. "Notes won't be deleted." moves from being buried in the message string to a calm block under it, because it is the single fact that changes the decision.
  • They animate out. ui/Modal already portals to document.body and locks scroll; it just never had an exit. Same 150ms kairos-modal-exit as every other dialog.
  • Deleting is now visibly connected to the list. Confirm, and the row it referred to collapses with dash-row-out and a toast rises. Try Delete selected in §1.
  • requireText stays unused. The primitive supports a type-the-name gate; notes has no operation destructive enough to warrant it, and this proposal does not add one.

Reduced motion

globals.css already zeroes durations wholesale under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce and adds targeted animation: none !important blocks per feature. Non-visual work this unblocks

Defects worth fixing in the same PR
  • Five duplicated focus traps. LockNoteDialog, RemoveLockDialog, PinResetDialog, NotebookDialog and ShareDialog each re-implement the same Tab-cycle + Escape + restoreTo effect. ui/Modal's useModalBehavior already does all of it, correctly, and adds body-scroll lock. Exit animations are the reason to consolidate now: an exit needs a mount/unmount delay, and writing that five times is how the five drift further apart.
  • Notes dialogs are not portalled. They render in place, inside a <main> that carries .kairos-page-enter — and that animation sets transform, which makes the main element a containing block for any position: fixed descendant. ui/Overlay exists (a 25-line createPortal) and is used by nothing in notes. This is a latent bug, not a stylistic preference.
  • Two overlay z-stacks. Mobile rail 50, ContextMenu 50, Share/Notebook 65, Lock/RemoveLock/PinReset 70, ui/Modal 100, Menu 30. Once everything portals through ui/Modal there is one ladder, matching the app-wide one (rail 40 · bars 50 · toasts 60 · modals 100).
  • loading.tsx omits kairos-bottomnav-gap and paints no topbar row, so the layout shifts on mount. One-line fix, visible on every cold load of the route.
  • CreateNoteForm.tsx is dead. Zero importers; it is the pre-rebuild inline creator, complete with a btn-primary gradient and a hand-rolled toggle switch. It should be deleted, not redesigned.
  • Copy already lives in en.json under notes.*, and every string in this document is quoted from it. No new keys are needed except for the two second lines added to the filter-miss empty states in §7.

Suggested sequence

Step 01 · plumbing
Move all five dialogs onto ui/Modal. Add the exit keyframes and the mount/unmount delay. No visual change ships yet — but every dialog gains a portal, one focus trap and an exit.
Step 02 · vocabulary
Add the micro-label, badge, button, chip and field classes. Apply them across notesUi.tsx, NoteList, NotesRail, NotePage and the dialogs. This is the bulk of the diff and it is mechanical.
Step 03 · motion
Apply the stagger, rise, pop, drawer and shimmer classes; fix loading.tsx; delete CreateNoteForm.tsx. Verify the whole surface with the Motion switch equivalent — OS reduced-motion on.
